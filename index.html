Our Family Legacy

The Northan Family Reunion celebrates the family and descendants of William “Big Bill” Northan Sr. and Minnie Snead Northan.

Their legacy continues through generations of children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, and extended family throughout the country.

This website will continue to grow as we preserve our family history, photographs, stories, genealogy, and memories for generations to come.
